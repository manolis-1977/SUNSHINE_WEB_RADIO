Privacy Policy

SunShine Web Radio is a static website hosted with GitHub Pages. The site does not currently require an account and does not directly operate an advertising or profiling system.

Local browser storage

Some preview features may store drafts or locally entered messages in your own browser using localStorage. These items stay on that device unless a future service explicitly states otherwise.

Live radio player

The live radio player is provided through Caster.fm. When you use the player, your browser connects to Caster.fm infrastructure and their own privacy terms may apply.

External resources

The site may load web fonts or embedded services from third-party providers. Those providers may receive standard technical request information such as IP address, browser type and request time.

Contact

The official public contact channel will be shown on the Contact page when it is activated.
